FAQ

Questions? Answered.

How much does it cost?

Every project is scoped individually on a kickoff call, and we quote a price for your specific project before any work begins. No hourly billing, no surprise invoices — just one price you agree to upfront.

What if my project is bigger than one core tool?

Tell us what you're building and we'll size it properly on the kickoff call and quote accordingly, whether that ends up being one core tool or something larger.

How fast can we start?

As soon as we scope the tool together on a kickoff call, usually within a few days of reaching out. Most projects go from kickoff to launch in a few weeks, depending on scope, with no proposal wait in between.

What's included in the one round of revisions?

After we build your tool, you get one structured round of feedback to fine-tune anything that's off before we call it done. Additional rounds beyond that are billed separately.

Do I own the code afterward?

Yes. You get full source code and documentation at handover. No lock-in, no retainer required to keep using what we built.

Is hosting included?

Yes, we deploy and configure hosting as part of your project, so your tool is live at launch, not just handed over as a folder of code.

What if The Build isn't the right fit for my business?

Happy to talk it through on a free call. If what you need is bigger than what fits in one build, we'll scope a custom project instead of forcing it into a box that doesn't fit.

Do you work with businesses outside your country?

Yes, most of our clients are remote, across the US, UK, Europe, and Australia. Everything runs async friendly with scheduled calls at times that work for your timezone.
