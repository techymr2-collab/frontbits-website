Is it a fit?

Built for some businesses. Not all of them.

We'd rather tell you on the first call than three weeks in. Here's who The Build works for, and who it doesn't.

A good fit if

  • You have one workflow that's clearly broken and worth fixing properly
  • You understand your own process better than any off-the-shelf tool does
  • You want to own what gets built outright, with no vendor holding the keys
  • You can look at a working build and give real feedback within a few days

Probably not us if

  • You need a multi-team platform with a year-long roadmap behind it
  • You want a developer embedded in your team month after month
  • You're counting on continuous feature work after launch — we hand over and step back
  • You're still working out what the tool should do — we scope one thing, we don't explore

Security & data

Your data stays yours.

We build tools that handle real business information — schedules, clients, contracts, money. Here's how we treat it, stated plainly.

It never lives with us

Your tool runs on your hosting, your database, your accounts. Your business data sits with you, not on a Frontbits server.

No access after handover

Any credentials we used during the build are yours to rotate at launch. We keep no standing access to your systems once the project is done.

Nothing tracking your users

No analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking baked into what we build for you, unless you specifically ask for it.

Being straight with you: we're a small studio, not an audited enterprise vendor. We hold no SOC 2 or ISO certification. If your industry needs formal compliance sign-off, raise it on the scoping call and we'll tell you honestly whether we can meet it.

FAQ

Questions? Answered.

How much does it cost?

Projects start from $999. Every project is scoped individually on a kickoff call, and we quote your specific price before any work begins. No hourly billing, no surprise invoices — just one price you agree to upfront.

What if my project is bigger than one core tool?

Tell us what you're building and we'll size it properly on the kickoff call and quote accordingly, whether that ends up being one core tool or something larger.

How fast can we start?

As soon as we scope the tool together on a kickoff call, usually within a few days of reaching out. Most projects go from kickoff to launch in a few weeks, depending on scope, with no proposal wait in between.

What's included in the one round of revisions?

After we build your tool, you get one structured round of feedback to fine-tune anything that's off before we call it done. Additional rounds beyond that are billed separately.

Do I own the code afterward?

Yes. You get full source code and documentation at handover. No lock-in, no retainer required to keep using what we built.

Is hosting included?

Yes, we deploy and configure hosting as part of your project, so your tool is live at launch, not just handed over as a folder of code.

What if The Build isn't the right fit for my business?

Happy to talk it through on a free call. If what you need is bigger than what fits in one build, we'll scope a custom project instead of forcing it into a box that doesn't fit.

Do you work with businesses outside your country?

Yes, most of our clients are remote, across the US, UK, Europe, and Australia. Everything runs async friendly with scheduled calls at times that work for your timezone.
