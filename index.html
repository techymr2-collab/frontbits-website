FAQ

Questions? Answered.

What exactly do I get for $5,000?

One core workflow or tool, scoped together on a kickoff call, up to 6 screens, one structured round of revisions, hosting and deployment, and full source code plus documentation at handover. That's the entire scope, fixed up front.

What if my project needs more than one tool or more than 6 screens?

That's outside The Build's fixed scope. Tell us what you're building and we'll scope a custom project instead, still fixed price, just sized to what you actually need.

How fast can we start?

As soon as we scope the tool together on a kickoff call, usually within a few days of reaching out. From kickoff to launch is a flat 4 weeks, no proposal wait in between.

What's included in the one round of revisions?

After we build your tool, you get one structured round of feedback to fine-tune anything that's off before we call it done. Additional rounds beyond that are billed separately.

Do I own the code afterward?

Yes. You get full source code and documentation at handover. No lock-in, no retainer required to keep using what we built.

Is hosting included?

Yes, we deploy and configure hosting as part of the fixed price, so your tool is live at launch, not just handed over as a folder of code.

What if The Build isn't the right fit for my business?

Happy to talk it through on a free call. If what you need is bigger than one fixed-scope tool, we'll scope a custom project instead of forcing it into a package that doesn't fit.

Do you work with businesses outside your country?

Yes, most of our clients are remote, across the US, UK, Europe, and Australia. Everything runs async friendly with scheduled calls at times that work for your timezone.
