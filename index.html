FAQ

Questions? Answered.

What is Frontbits?

Frontbits is an all-in-one business operations platform for startups and small teams: proposals, contracts, invoicing, payments, time tracking, and client CRM, all in one place instead of five disconnected tools.

Why is it waitlist-only instead of self-serve signup?

We're still building the product and onboarding early customers in small batches so we can get it right. Joining the waitlist gets you an invite as soon as a spot opens.

When can I expect access?

We're inviting early users in waves over the coming months. Join the waitlist now and we'll email you as soon as it's your turn.

How will pricing work?

Frontbits will launch with three subscription tiers — Starter, Growth, and Scale — priced monthly. Exact pricing is being finalized, and waitlist members will hear about it, and get access, before it's public.

What does the platform actually include?

Proposals and contracts, invoicing and online payments, time tracking, and a full client CRM, plus a dashboard that ties it all together. See the Features section above for the full breakdown.

I need something built right now, not later. What do I do?

Check out The Build, our separate fixed-price custom software service: one core tool for your business, live in 4 weeks, $1,999 flat.

Is my data secure?

Yes. We use reputable, industry-standard providers to store and process data, and only collect what's needed to run the waitlist and, later, the product itself. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Do you work with businesses outside your country?

Yes, Frontbits is built for startups and small teams anywhere — the waitlist and, at launch, the platform itself are open to businesses across the US, UK, Europe, Australia, and beyond.
