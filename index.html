Is it a fit?

Built for some businesses. Not all of them.

We'd rather tell you on the first call than halfway through the build. Here's who The Build works for, and who it doesn't.

A good fit if

  • You have one workflow that's clearly broken and worth fixing properly
  • You understand your own process better than any off-the-shelf tool does
  • You want to own what gets built outright, with no vendor holding the keys
  • You can look at a working build and give real feedback within a few days

Probably not us if

  • You need a multi-team platform with a year-long roadmap behind it
  • You want a developer embedded in your team month after month
  • You're counting on continuous feature work after launch, but we hand over and step back
  • You're still working out what the tool should do. We scope one thing, we don't explore
After launch

You leave with everything. Including your data.

We hand the project over completely and step back. Here’s exactly what that means, stated plainly.

What you get

  • Full source code and documentation, written to be picked up by any competent developer, not just by us
  • Hosting and database live in infrastructure you own from day one, not ours
  • No retainer, no lock-in, no ongoing access kept on our side once the project is done

How we treat your data

  • Your business data sits in your accounts, not on a Frontbits server
  • Any credentials we used during the build are yours to rotate at launch; we keep no standing access afterward
  • No analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking baked in, unless you specifically ask for it

Being straight with you: we’re a small studio, not an audited enterprise vendor. We hold no SOC 2 or ISO certification. If your industry needs formal compliance sign-off, raise it on the scoping call and we’ll tell you honestly whether we can meet it.

FAQ

Questions? Answered.

How much does it cost?

There's no set number until we've talked. A typical build is four modules with full CRUD and the workflow that connects them, one or two integrations with tools you already use, as many screens as those modules need, one round of revisions, hosting and deployment, and the source code. We size all of that on the kickoff call and give you one number before any work starts. No hourly billing, no surprise invoices.

What if my project is bigger than one core tool?

Tell us what you're building and we'll size it properly on the kickoff call and quote accordingly, whether that ends up being the typical four-module build or something larger.

How fast can we start?

As soon as we agree the scope and price on a kickoff call, usually within a few days of reaching out. You get your number on that call rather than waiting on a proposal, and the base build goes from kickoff to launch in about a week.

What's included in the one round of revisions?

After we build your dashboard, you get one structured round of feedback to fine-tune anything that's off before we call it done. That round is included in your price. Additional rounds, and anything outside the agreed scope, are billed separately.

Do I own the code afterward?

Yes. You get full source code and documentation at handover. No lock-in, no retainer required to keep using what we built.

Is hosting included?

Yes, we deploy and configure hosting as part of your project, so your tool is live at launch, not just handed over as a folder of code.

What if The Build isn't the right fit for my business?

Happy to talk it through on a free call. If what you need is bigger than the base build, we will tell you plainly and price it properly rather than forcing it into a box that doesn't fit.

Do you work with businesses outside your country?

Yes, most of our clients are remote, across the US, UK, Europe, and Australia. Everything runs async friendly with scheduled calls at times that work for your timezone.
