Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

Frontbits LLC ("Frontbits," "we," "us," or "our") is registered in the Shams Free Zone, United Arab Emirates. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through frontbits.com (the "Site"), how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the Site, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

We collect information directly from you and, to a limited extent, automatically through your use of the Site.

Information you provide

  • Signup form: name, email address, and company (if provided), submitted through the signup form on the Site.
  • Contact form: name, email address, project type, and project details you submit through the form on the Site.
  • Booking a call: when you schedule a call through our booking widget, we receive your name, email address, and any details you provide about your project or availability.
  • Email correspondence: anything you send us directly at hello@frontbits.com.

Information collected automatically

Our hosting provider logs standard technical data for security and reliability purposes, such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited. We do not run analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies of our own on the Site.

2. How we use your information

  • To set up your account and notify you when access is ready
  • To respond to your inquiry and evaluate whether your project fits our fixed-scope package
  • To schedule and prepare for calls you book with us
  • To communicate with you about your project, including by email
  • To keep the Site secure and functioning correctly

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising or marketing to third parties.

3. Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to run the Site and our business. Each processes data only as needed to provide their service to us:

  • Formspree — delivers signup and contact form submissions to us by email.
  • Cal.com — powers our call-booking widget and calendar invites.
  • Vercel — hosts the Site and serves its files.

These providers may process your data outside the UAE. We do not share your information with any other third party except where required by law.

4. Cookies

Frontbits does not set analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. Third-party tools embedded on the Site (currently, the Cal.com booking widget) may set their own cookies to function — those are governed by that provider's own privacy policy.

5. Data retention

We keep contact form submissions and booking details for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry, deliver a project, and maintain our business records, after which we delete or anonymize it unless we're required to keep it longer for legal reasons.

6. Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by emailing hello@frontbits.com. We'll respond within a reasonable timeframe.

7. Data security

We use reputable, industry-standard providers (listed above) to store and transmit your information, and we limit access to it to what's needed to run our business. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

8. Children's privacy

The Site is intended for businesses and professionals. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and the Site is not directed at anyone under 18.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the Site after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.

10. Governing law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws applicable in the Shams Free Zone, United Arab Emirates, without regard to conflict-of-law principles.

11. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at hello@frontbits.com.
