The challenge

Harbor & Pine handled customer support out of a single shared inbox. As orders grew, so did the pile — messages got answered twice or not at all, urgent issues sat behind routine ones, and no one could say how long a customer had been waiting.

The team was working hard, but the inbox gave them no way to see what actually needed attention first.

The approach

We built a proper support desk: every message becomes a ticket with an owner, a priority, and a response clock, so the team always knows what to pick up next.

  • A shared queue where every ticket has one clear owner
  • Priority and SLA timers, so urgent issues rise to the top automatically
  • Customer history on every ticket, so no one starts from scratch

The result

First response time dropped from hours to minutes, nothing falls through the cracks anymore, and the team handles a growing order volume without the inbox chaos growing with it.
