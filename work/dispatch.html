The challenge

Northaire Mechanical ran 28 technicians off a whiteboard in the office and a busy group chat. The dispatcher spent every morning on the phone working out who was free, who was nearby, and which jobs were still open.

Customers calling to ask when a technician would arrive got an estimate, not an answer, and jobs occasionally slipped through the gap between the board and the chat.

The approach

We built one dispatch board where every job for the day lives, gets assigned to a technician, and moves through clear stages the whole office can see.

  • A live board of the day’s jobs, from unassigned through to done
  • One-tap assignment to the right technician, with their status visible at a glance
  • Real job status — open, en route, on site, done — instead of a guess

The result

The morning phone-around disappeared, more jobs got done in a day because routing was tighter, and the office could finally tell a customer where their technician actually was.
