The Marlowe

Real-time front-desk operations for a 40-room boutique hotel

Boutique hotel Front-desk operations tool 1 week, kickoff to launch

The challenge

The Marlowe is a 40-room boutique property where occupancy, ADR, and RevPAR were tracked in a spreadsheet updated once a night, so the numbers the front desk was working from were always yesterday's. Housekeeping status lived on a paper board updated over radio, arrivals and departures got cross-referenced by hand against the PMS export, and an out-of-order room could sit off inventory for days before anyone followed up on the ticket.

Guest notes, repeat-stay history, and VIP flags were scattered across the reservation system and staff memory, so a returning guest's preferences didn't always make it to whoever was at the desk that shift.

The approach

We built one front-desk screen: tonight's occupancy, ADR, and RevPAR live against the same night last week, today's arrivals and departures with room readiness and guest notes attached, a needs-attention feed surfacing what's actually urgent, a full room-status grid across every floor, and maintenance tickets tied to the specific room they affect.

  • Live occupancy, ADR, and RevPAR compared against the same night last week, not a once-a-day export
  • Today's arrivals and departures with room readiness and guest notes (VIP status, repeat stays, preferences) in one list
  • A needs-attention feed surfacing overdue housekeeping, unassigned reservations, and sell-out alerts before they become a problem at check-in
  • Full room-status grid across every floor, filterable by vacant, dirty, cleaning, occupied, or out of order
  • Maintenance tickets tied to specific rooms, so an out-of-order room doesn't quietly stay off inventory

The result

The front desk now works from tonight's real numbers instead of yesterday's export, housekeeping status updates the moment a room turns instead of over radio, and every out-of-order room has a ticket someone's accountable for instead of sitting forgotten. Guest notes travel with the reservation, so a repeat guest's preferences show up no matter who's on shift.
