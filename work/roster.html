The challenge

Store managers were building weekly schedules in separate spreadsheets, then texting shift swaps back and forth whenever someone needed coverage. Head office had no visibility into staffing until someone didn't show up.

Every location did it a little differently, which meant no manager could easily cover for another, and every schedule started from scratch.

The approach

We built one scheduling tool shared across every location, so managers build shifts against real availability and staff can request swaps without a text thread.

  • A shared schedule view across all locations, built for managers in minutes instead of hours
  • Self-serve shift swap requests, routed to a manager for one-tap approval
  • A live coverage view so gaps show up before the shift starts, not during it

The result

What used to take a manager most of a Sunday afternoon now takes under an hour, and no-shows dropped once staff could see and trade shifts themselves instead of texting around.
